About Zinos Wealth

Independent advice.
Focused on you.

We're a Queensland-based financial advisory practice dedicated to helping individuals, families and business owners achieve financial clarity and long-term security.

Our story

Advice built on
relationships.

Zinos Wealth was founded on a simple belief: that good financial advice should be personal, transparent and genuinely in your interest. Not a product sold to you, but a partnership built around your life.

We take the time to understand what matters most to you — your goals, your family, your concerns — before we recommend anything. From there, we create a tailored strategy to help you build wealth, protect what you have, and plan for the future with confidence.

Whether you're just starting out, approaching retirement, or somewhere in between, our role is to bring clarity to your decisions and walk alongside you as your circumstances change.
