What's involved

The right cover —
and the right structure.

We help you understand which cover you actually need, and how much, so you're neither dangerously under-insured nor paying for protection that doesn't fit your life.

Getting the structure right matters as much as the cover itself — including whether policies are best held inside or outside superannuation for cost and tax efficiency.
